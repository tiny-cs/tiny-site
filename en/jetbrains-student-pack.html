JetBrains Student Pack: All IDEs Free for a Year, Renewable While Enrolled

The JetBrains Student Pack is a free license for enrolled students: verify your student status once and use JetBrains' full set of professional IDEs free — including IntelliJ IDEA Ultimate, PyCharm Professional, WebStorm, GoLand, CLion and 10 IDEs in total — plus hands-on programming courses in JetBrains Academy. Renewable every year while you're a student, at no cost.

Two things to be clear about: first, the Student Pack covers the IDEs themselves, not the paid JetBrains AI Pro subscription — students only get the AI free tier, plus one 30-day AI Pro trial. Second, you must be enrolled full-time in a program of at least one year; short online courses, bootcamps and training programs don't qualify.

Eligibility

  • Enrolled full-time at an accredited school: high school, college and graduate school all count; the program must last at least one year
  • Able to prove student status, any one of three ways: school email, ISIC / ITIC international student card, or a GitHub Student Pack account
  • A school email is fastest; if your school email isn't on JetBrains' approved list, use the GitHub Student Pack or ISIC instead
  • Short online courses, bootcamps and training programs don't qualify and will be rejected

Step 1: Prepare Your Verification Documents

Pick one of three and have it ready: a school email (one that can receive verification mail), your ISIC / ITIC card number, or an approved GitHub Student Pack account. Don't have a GitHub Student Pack yet? See our tutorial: GitHub Student Pack: 80+ Developer Perks with One Verification — once you have it, you can use it to verify JetBrains too.

Step 2: Open the Student Pack Page and Apply

Open https://www.jetbrains.com/academy/student-pack/ and click "Apply for Student Pack" / "Request now", then fill in your school, name and verification method as prompted. With a school email, enter it and confirm the verification email; with the GitHub Student Pack or ISIC, link your account as the page instructs.

Step 3: Wait for Review, Then Sign In and Download

School-email verification is usually fast; manual review takes a few days. Once approved, sign in to your JetBrains account, download the IDEs you need from the official site, and sign in with the same account to activate — no activation code needed.

Note: your name, school and enrollment date must match your documents exactly. If rejected, switch to another verification method and resubmit as prompted — no need to register a new account.

Step 4: Renew Every Year

The license lasts one year. JetBrains emails a reminder two weeks before expiry; click the renewal link in your JetBrains account and re-verify. As long as you're still enrolled, you can renew every year. After graduation you can't renew, but the graduation discount still applies — 40% off products for 2 years.

FAQ

Is AI Pro free?
No. The Student Pack only includes the IDEs, not the paid AI Pro subscription. Students get the AI free tier, plus one 30-day AI Pro trial.

What if I don't have a school email?
Use a GitHub Student Pack account or an ISIC / ITIC card to verify — it works the same. Same if your school email domain isn't on the approved list; switch to one of these two methods.

Can bootcamp / online-course students apply?
No. Only full-time programs of at least one year qualify; short courses, bootcamps and training programs don't.

How many computers can I install it on?
Multiple. Like a regular personal subscription, one student license can be used on multiple devices.

Can I use it for commercial projects?
No. The student license is for non-commercial educational use only, including coursework and academic research.

Does it conflict with the GitHub Student Pack?
No. The GitHub Student Pack itself includes a JetBrains license, and it can also serve as verification material for JetBrains — claiming both is the best deal.
