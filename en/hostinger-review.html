Black Friday Sale: The Honest Truth First

The Black Friday sale runs from October 19 to December 14, 2026. Black Friday itself is November 27, and Cyber Monday is November 30. But the VPS Black Friday prices are the same as the regular prices: KVM 1 was $6.49/month in August, and it is still $6.49/month on Black Friday — only the banner changed, not the price. The "67% off" claim is compared against a $19.49 list price, not against the previous selling price.

Shared hosting is where the real discount is: Premium is $1.99/month, but you have to prepay 48 months at once ($95.52), and it renews at $10.99/month.

Plans of 12 months or longer include a free domain for one year, and shared hosting includes free email. The low prices all require a one-time 4-year prepayment, and renewals cost 4–5 times the intro price — do the full math before buying.

Pros

  • Cheap entry: the VPS starts with 4GB of RAM, with AMD EPYC + NVMe.
  • The hPanel control panel is beginner-friendly, and setting up a WordPress site is easy.
  • Datacenters in the US, Europe, India, Indonesia, Malaysia and Brazil — good for e-commerce sites and English blogs.
  • 30-day refund; VPS refunds are limited to once per 180 days.
  • Accepts Alipay, credit cards and other payment methods.

Cons

  • Renewal prices jump a lot — KVM 4 more than doubles on renewal, so calculate costs at the renewal price for long-term use.
  • No datacenters in Hong Kong, Singapore, Japan or South Korea, and no CN2 GIA or other China-optimized routes. Not recommended for websites whose visitors are mainly in China.
  • The VPS is self-managed: you maintain the operating system and security yourself, so beginners need to learn some Linux.
  • Low prices require prepaying 24 months (VPS) or 48 months (shared hosting) at once, which ties up a lot of money.

Who It's For — and Who It's Not For

Good for: e-commerce sites, English blogs, and websites for users outside China; people who want a VPS for n8n, AI tools, or lightweight apps; beginners on a budget who can prepay for two years.

Not good for: websites whose readers are mainly in China; people who don't want to manage a server themselves and just want to launch with a few clicks; people who want to pay monthly and don't want to prepay for two years.

Hostinger vs BandwagonHost vs RackNerd

If you want the fastest access from China with good routes, choose BandwagonHost CN2 GIA. If you want to spend just over $20 a year with no renewal price increase, choose RackNerd. If you want more RAM, easy website building, and an audience outside China, choose Hostinger. All three are listed in our paid recommendations — pick based on where your readers are and what you'll use it for.

Reminder: before buying, confirm the billing cycle and the renewal price — the checkout page clearly states "renews at $xx/mo". Don't just look at the big $1.99 or $6.49 figures. If you're not satisfied within 30 days, you can get a refund — don't let the deadline pass.
