Google Student Verification: Get Google AI Pro / AI Plus Free for a Year

Google launched a student offer on August 19, 2026: currently enrolled college students aged 18 or older can get 12 months of Google's paid AI plans free after verifying their student status with SheerID. You must claim it before December 31, 2026 — no extensions.

Let's be clear about who can claim it and who can't: which tier you get depends on the country/region where your school is, not your nationality. Currently enrolled students in the US get Google AI Pro (normally $19.99/month); students in 140+ other markets get Google AI Plus (a cheaper tier). Mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau, Canada, Bolivia, Albania, and Tunisia are not included — if you study there, you can't claim it. Students in Taiwan fall under the AI Plus tier. Chinese students studying abroad are judged by where their school is.

The 12 months start on the day you claim, not on December 31 — so claiming on December 31 still gives you access until December 31, 2027.

Eligibility (All Required)

  • Aged 18 or older, with age verification completed on your Google account
  • Currently enrolled at a higher-education institution (undergraduate or graduate; high school students don't qualify, and an admission letter alone doesn't count — SheerID checks current enrollment status)
  • Sign in with a personal @gmail.com account; a school-issued Workspace for Education account won't work
  • A payment method (credit/debit card) must be linked when you sign up — no charge for the first 12 months
  • Your Google Play country (Android/web) or payment profile country must match the country where you live and study
  • Not part of someone else's Google One family group; Google One must not be paid for through a third party such as Google Fi or a carrier
  • Students whose 2025 trial has expired can claim again, but you must re-verify every year

Step 1: Open the Claim Page and Sign In with Your Personal Account

Open https://one.google.com/ai-student and sign in with the personal Gmail account you want to use. You can also enter from the Gemini students page https://gemini.google/students — both lead to the same offer.

Note: never sign in to your Google account with your school email. School-issued education accounts aren't eligible — sign in with a personal Gmail account and verify your student status separately through SheerID.

Step 2: Verify Your Student Status with SheerID

After clicking claim, you'll be taken to a SheerID (third-party verification service) form. Fill in:

  • Name: exactly as it appears on your school documents — even one letter off can get it rejected
  • Birthday: matching the birthday on your Google account
  • School: select your university; the system matches it automatically
  • School email: if asked, use your school email to receive a verification message and click to confirm

Most people pass after filling in the form and confirming via their school email. If automatic verification fails, SheerID will ask you to upload proof of enrollment — for example a photo of your student ID, an enrollment letter on school letterhead, or a tuition bill. Upload a clear photo of the original, not a cropped screenshot.

Step 3: Add a Payment Method

After verification, add a card as prompted. It's only used for auto-renewal after the free period — no charge for the first 12 months. You can't claim without a card; it's a hard requirement.

Step 4: Confirm the Plan Is Active

Go back to the Google One or Gemini page. If your plan now shows AI Pro (US) or AI Plus (other markets) with an expiry date 12 months after your claim date, it worked. Try an advanced model in Gemini to confirm you're on the paid quota.

Step 5: Set a Reminder and Decide Before Renewal

The free period auto-renews at full price: Pro at $19.99/month, Plus at $4.99/month or the local price. To cancel: Google One → Settings → Cancel membership. After cancelling, the free period runs until its end date — it doesn't stop immediately.

You also need to re-verify with SheerID every year to keep the student price, and you can't re-verify after graduation.

FAQ

Is it really free? Will I be charged first?
No charge for the first 12 months, but you must link a card. It auto-renews at expiry, so remember to cancel.

Can students in mainland China claim it?
No. Mainland China isn't in the sales regions for Google AI plans, and Hong Kong and Macau are also on the exclusion list. You can't pass verification with a school in mainland China.

Can Chinese students studying in the US claim it?
Yes. What matters is where your school is, not your nationality. Proof of enrollment at a US school + US payment details gets you AI Pro.

What if verification fails?
The most common causes are a name that doesn't match your documents, a birthday that doesn't match your Google account, or signing in with a school Workspace account. Fix them and resubmit; if it still fails, upload proof of enrollment for manual review as prompted.

I claimed the 2025 trial before — can I claim again?
Yes. Google says students whose 2025 trial has expired can claim this one.

Can high school students or newly admitted students claim it?
No. You must be 18 or older and currently enrolled; an admission letter doesn't count.
