Pros

  • Cheap: 1 CPU core / 1GB RAM / 20GB SSD / 3TB traffic for $21.99 per year, with the same price on renewal.
  • Renewals don't go up in price, which is more honest than many providers that run first-year specials.
  • Many locations — 20 datacenters, including Los Angeles, San Jose, New York and Ashburn.
  • Los Angeles DC02 is optimized for China Unicom; in evening peak-hour tests from China, a single thread reached over 300Mbps.
  • Datacenter changes and OS reinstalls are available in the control panel, and the panel is not complicated.

Cons

  • Performance depends on your ISP: good on China Unicom, average on China Telecom, and poor on China Mobile — China Mobile users should choose carefully.
  • It's a budget VPS; don't expect the stability and support response times of a major cloud provider.
  • Special-price plans are often out of stock, and popular tiers require waiting for restocks.
  • Support is ticket-only — no live chat.

How to Choose a Datacenter

  • China Unicom users: Los Angeles DC02 first — it performs best during evening peak hours.
  • China Telecom users: Los Angeles DC02 works too, but don't expect too much. You can buy one month first and then renew yearly if it works for you.
  • China Mobile users: not recommended — noticeable lag in our tests.
  • For websites and services: pick the datacenter closest to your visitors, rather than assuming Los Angeles is always best.
