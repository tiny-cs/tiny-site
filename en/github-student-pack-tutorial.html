GitHub Student Pack: 80+ Developer Perks with One Verification

The GitHub Student Developer Pack is GitHub's free bundle for students: verify your student status once and get free perks from GitHub itself and about 80 partners, including Copilot, GitHub Pro, cloud server credits, dev tools and free domains. It costs nothing and stays valid while you're a student.

Two things to be clear about: first, the partner perks change often — this article lists the items checked in October 2026; go by what the official pack page shows when you claim. Second, after the Copilot Student revamp in March 2026, new activations were paused for a while — whether you can activate it depends on what the official site shows when you apply; don't trust anyone who says it's "guaranteed."

Eligibility

  • Aged 13 or older, with a personal GitHub account (organization accounts don't work)
  • Currently enrolled in a degree or diploma program: high school, college, graduate school and homeschooling all count
  • Able to prove enrollment: a school email is fastest; without one, upload a photo of your student ID, enrollment letter or transcript
  • The Azure $100 credit requires you to be 18 or older

Step 1: Prepare a Personal GitHub Account

If you don't have one, register at github.com. Pick your username carefully — you'll use it for claiming perks and writing code. Use your real name and school so they match your documents and verification goes faster.

Step 2: Open the Student Pack Page and Apply

Open https://education.github.com/pack and click "Sign up for Student Developer Pack" (you can also enter from the Education section in GitHub Settings). Follow the prompts to select your school and fill in your enrollment details.

Step 3: Verify Your Student Status

Fastest with a school email: enter it, receive the verification email and click to confirm — usually approved quickly.

Without a school email: upload proof of enrollment — photos of both sides of your student ID, an enrollment letter, or a transcript on school letterhead all work. Take a clear photo; don't upload a cropped screenshot. Review usually takes a few days, most within about 72 hours.

Note: your name, school and enrollment date must match your documents exactly — even one letter off can get it sent back. If rejected, follow the instructions to add documents and resubmit; no need to register again.

Step 4: Claim Perks One by One After Approval

Go back to the pack page and claim each perk by following its prompts. Start with these: GitHub Pro, Copilot Student, Codespaces, Azure $100, JetBrains, and the free domain. The rest can wait until you need them — they'll still be there.

FAQ

Can high school students apply?
Yes. Anyone 13+ enrolled in a degree or diploma program qualifies, including high school. Only the Azure $100 credit requires you to be 18.

What if I don't have a school email?
Upload your student ID or enrollment letter for manual review. It works the same, just slower.

Can I actually get Copilot?
After the March 2026 student revamp, new activations were paused for a while. Whether you can activate it depends on what the official site shows when you apply. If you can't get it, use Copilot Free for now — the other perks aren't affected.

Do the perks stay after graduation?
No. Once you graduate and lose student status, the perks expire one by one, and yearly ones like JetBrains can't be renewed.

Does it conflict with Google student verification?
No. One is GitHub's developer perks, the other is Google's AI plan — you can claim both. Google's offer ends December 31, 2026; see our tutorial: Google Student Verification: Get Google AI Pro / AI Plus Free for a Year.
