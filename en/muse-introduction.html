How to Join

Free or Paid

The free plan works fine, with a usage allowance that refreshes each cycle. Paid plans give you more usage and run the stronger Muse Wonder model, suited to people who rely on it for long tasks every day. Try the free plan for a few days first, and only consider paying if you find you can't live without it.
