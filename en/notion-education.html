Notion Education: Free Plus Plan for Students, Re-verify Once a Year

Notion Education is a free perk for enrolled college students and teachers: verify once with your school email, and a one-member workspace is upgraded to the Plus plan for free — unlimited pages, unlimited uploads, and 30-day version history. No student ID to submit, no forms to fill — if your school is on the approved list, it goes through automatically.

Three things to be clear about first: first, the Education plan is only for one-member workspaces — a workspace with multiple members can't claim it; second, verification only accepts a school email, not a student ID or any other document; third, the Education plan does not include full Notion AI — only a limited AI trial. For the full AI suite, you need to upgrade to the Business plan separately.

The Plus plan normally costs a monthly fee, so the Education plan is essentially free. The 30-day version history is great for writing essays: if you delete a paragraph by mistake or break a draft, you can recover it within a month. Unlimited uploads let you keep lecture slides, essay PDFs, and research materials in one workspace instead of hunting for files everywhere.

Eligibility

  • Full-time students or teachers at an accredited college or university, with the school on the World Higher Education Database (WHED) approved list. It's not just about a .edu domain — school emails from many countries work.
  • Your Notion account's primary email must be your school email. A personal email like Gmail won't work, and a student ID, admission letter, or other documents can't replace email verification.
  • The workspace you claim it for must have only you in it, and must be on the Free plan first. If you're already on paid Plus, downgrade back to Free and remove other members before claiming.
  • K-12 students and teachers can't claim this personal Education plan — only the regular Free plan.
  • One school email can claim it once, for one workspace only. Once you upgrade that workspace to a paid Plus, Business, or Enterprise plan, your Education eligibility is permanently lost — even if you later downgrade back to Free, it won't come back.

Step 1: Change Your Account Email to Your School Email

Open Notion on desktop or web, click Settings at the bottom left → My Account → Email, change your primary email to your school email and confirm it via the email you receive. Note that it must be your primary email — adding a secondary email doesn't count. If you don't have a Notion account yet, register one with your school email at https://www.notion.com/.

Step 2: Claim the Free Education Plan in Settings

Do this on desktop or web as well — you can't claim it in the mobile app. Click Settings at the bottom left → Upgrade plan, scroll down to the section for students and teachers, and click Get free education plan. If your school is on Notion's list, the upgrade happens automatically with no manual review.

If you can't find this option, check two things first: whether your workspace has only you in it, and whether it's on the Free plan. If both are correct and you still can't see it, your school's domain may not be on Notion's list yet — you can email edu@makenotion.com and ask them to add your school's domain.

Step 3: Re-verify Once a Year

The Education plan has no fixed expiry date, but you need to re-verify your school email once a year. Notion will email you a reminder before it's due — just follow the instructions to re-verify. After graduation, when your school email stops working, you can't claim it anymore. You can then downgrade back to the Free plan or subscribe to Plus at the regular price.

What About Student Clubs?

The personal Education plan is for one person only. Clubs and student organizations that want to use it together need to apply separately for the student organization Education plan: a multi-member workspace is free, with the same unlimited uploads, 30-day version history, and up to 100 guests — but you need to submit an application and pass verification. Claim your personal plan first, then the club applies separately.

FAQ

What if I don't have a school email?
You can't claim it. Notion only accepts a school email and doesn't take student IDs, admission letters, or other proof. Ask your school whether it can issue you a student email.

Is Notion AI free?
No. The Education plan gives you the Plus plan, which doesn't include full Notion AI — only a limited AI trial. Full AI (Agent, meeting notes, enterprise search) requires the Business plan at $20 per member per month.

I'm already on paid Plus. Can I still claim it?
Yes, but first downgrade back to the Free plan and remove other members from the workspace, then claim it following Step 2 above. Before downgrading, make sure all your important content is still there.

Can I use it with classmates?
The workspace can only have you as a member, but you can add up to 100 guests to collaborate — classmates and teachers can join as guests to edit pages and leave comments.

Does it conflict with the GitHub Student Pack?
No. The GitHub Student Pack also includes a Notion perk — both channels give you the same kind of Education plan, so don't claim it twice. Claiming it directly with your school email is the fastest. Our tutorial: GitHub Student Pack: 80+ Developer Perks with One Verification.
