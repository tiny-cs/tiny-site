Student Discounts: Spotify, Prime and YouTube Student Prices in One Place

Your student status doesn't just get you free AI and developer tools — music, video and shopping, those fixed monthly costs, can be cut in half too. The table below shows US prices; prices differ by country, so check the local price on each service's student page before signing up.

Three things to be clear about first: first, these are discounts, not free — you still pay every month, so don't confuse them with the free perks; second, most of them require you to re-verify your student status every year, and if you forget, the price quietly goes back to full price; third, prices went up in another round in 2026, so the numbers below are subject to each provider's official site.

How to Get Spotify Student

Open https://www.spotify.com/student/, claim the student offer and let SheerID verify you: enter your school name and graduation year — if it can be looked up automatically it takes seconds; if not, upload your student ID, proof of enrollment or transcript, and you'll usually get a result in 24–48 hours. After verification it's $6.99 a month, and in the US Hulu (with ads) is included. You have to re-verify every year — if you forget, it quietly goes back to $12.99, so set a reminder on your phone calendar.

How to Get Amazon Prime Student

Open https://www.amazon.com/primestudent and verify with a school email or SheerID. The first 6 months are completely free, then $7.49/mo or $69/yr, including all Prime benefits like Prime Video and free delivery, until graduation or 4 years, when it automatically switches back to full price. Note: the free 6 months auto-renews, so cancel before it ends if you don't want it.

YouTube Premium and Apple Music

YouTube Premium Student is claimed at https://www.youtube.com/premium/student with SheerID verification, $8.99/mo, covering no ads, background play, downloads and YouTube Music. Apple Music Student is claimed on Apple's website with UNiDAYS verification, $6.99/mo, and includes Apple TV+. Both last up to 4 years and switch back to full price automatically after graduation.

UNiDAYS and Student Beans: Discount Aggregators

Beyond the services above, you can also sign up for UNiDAYS (https://www.myunidays.com) and Student Beans (https://www.studentbeans.com), both free: verify once with a school email or proof of enrollment and you'll see student discounts from hundreds of brands — for example 10% off Nike or ASOS. The two platforms carry different brands, so sign up for both and compare prices. They also have browser extensions that automatically remind you at checkout if there's a student discount available.

To be clear about what has no student discount: Netflix has no student price, and neither does YouTube TV — don't believe posts online claiming a "Netflix student price."

FAQ

What do I need for verification?
Usually a school email, or a student ID, proof of enrollment, transcript or tuition bill. SheerID and UNiDAYS both accept these, and manual review usually takes 24–48 hours.

Can I keep using it after graduation?
No. After graduation or a failed verification, the subscription automatically switches back to full price — keep paying at full price if you want to continue, or cancel.

Can international students in China get these?
It depends on the region and school. Student pricing for Spotify, YouTube and Prime is available in the US, Canada, the UK, Australia and most European countries; schools in mainland China are not on the list. Chinese students studying abroad claim under the policy of the country they're studying in.

Do these discounts conflict with the free perks?
No. The free AI and developer tools are claimed as usual, and music and video are paid at student prices — the two don't interfere with each other.
