Figma Education: Free Professional Plan for Students, 3,000 AI Credits a Month

Figma Education is a free perk for enrolled students and teachers: verify your student status once and you can use Figma Professional for free, including Figma Design, FigJam, Slides, and Dev Mode, with unlimited files and projects plus team libraries. Higher-education and approved bootcamp students also get 3,000 AI credits a month for AI tools like Figma Make.

Three things to be clear about first: first, register a Figma account before applying — the account email must be a school email, and personal emails are automatically rejected; second, your education status is tied to the email you verified with — changing your email after verification cancels your eligibility immediately; third, AI credits are only for higher-education and bootcamp students — high school and K-12 users get the design tools only, with no AI features.

The Professional plan normally costs a monthly fee (a Full seat is $16 a month billed annually), so the Education plan is essentially free. Note: different official pages state the validity period differently — some say 2 years, some say 6 months for bootcamps. Eligibility

  • You're a current student or teacher taking classes in a classroom: universities, high schools, approved online courses and bootcamps, and design or coding schools all count. Self-directed learning, startups, edtech companies, and non-profits don't.
  • You're old enough to consent to using the service on your own, without a parent signing for you. Non-teaching staff at a school don't qualify.
  • Apply with a school email — personal emails are automatically rejected. If your school doesn't have an approved email domain, you can upload enrollment proof such as a student ID, enrollment letter, or transcript. Don't upload a class schedule.
  • The Education plan is only available in select regions, and Figma hasn't published the full list. Some countries and regions may not be eligible — if you're unsure, email education@figma.com to ask.
  • Bootcamp students don't apply on their own: the bootcamp admin first joins Figma's trusted partner list, then verifies students in bulk. The bootcamp needs a structured mentored curriculum, at least 10 students per cohort, a public website with its courses and instructors, and non-commercial use.

Step 1: Register a Figma Account with Your School Email

Register the account first — don't apply before that, the order matters. Open https://www.figma.com/ and sign up with your school email. If you already have an account under a personal email, change the account email to your school email before applying. Your education status is tied to this email, and changing it after verification cancels your eligibility, so use the account you plan to keep using long-term.

Step 2: Submit Your Application on the Education Page

Open https://www.figma.com/education/apply and pick your school type: Higher Ed for university students, High school for high school students. Follow the prompts to fill in your school, name, and email. Higher-ed verification usually goes through SheerID, and you'll get an email within a few days. Don't use a VPN when applying, and upload proof that shows you're enrolled — not a class schedule. If SheerID rejects you, contact SheerID support first instead of resubmitting over and over.

K-12 comes in two forms: for personal use, apply directly on the page above; if a school wants all its students to use it, the school applies through an agreement, and the Enterprise plan is managed by the school or district. Students under 13 also go through the school route.

Step 3: Wait for Approval, Then Reapply Before It Expires

If your school email matches, approval is usually quick. Once approved, your account becomes an Education account and you can create an education team to use the Professional features. Just reapply before it expires — if you're still enrolled, it renews. Note: if it lapses without renewal, the team falls back to the free Starter plan; teams with more than 3 files get locked and you can only download or move files, so make sure to renew in advance.

FAQ

What if I don't have a school email?
Ask your school whether it can issue you a student email first. If there's really no approved domain, you can upload a student ID, enrollment letter, or transcript as enrollment proof when applying, and Figma will review it manually.

How do the AI credits work?
Higher-ed and bootcamp students get 3,000 credits a month, shared across all AI features. Figma Make, AI drafts, and more all use credits — once they're gone for the month, that's it until next month. High school and K-12 users get no AI credits.

Can I use it for commercial projects?
No. The Education plan is only for learning and classroom projects — not for paid client work.

Can I still use it after graduation?
No. After it expires you need to reapply, and only students still enrolled can renew. After graduation you fall back to the free Starter plan, so export your important files in advance.

Does it conflict with the GitHub Student Pack?
No. The GitHub Student Pack doesn't include Figma — the two are separate, claim each one on its own. Our tutorial: GitHub Student Pack: 80+ Developer Perks with One Verification.
